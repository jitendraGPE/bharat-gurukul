# Bharat: Rooted in Heritage, Inspired for the Future — the plan

*Ancient Wisdom for Modern Excellence*

Twelve Sunday-morning gatherings in 2027, from the Vedas to the Buddha, with the Ramayana and Mahabharata at the heart of the year. Free for everyone, and run entirely by volunteers: no fees, no sponsors, only seva. Each gathering is opened by invited delegates, presented by a volunteer, and ends with a certificate for everyone who took part.

## Why we gather

A better person makes a better family, a stronger community and a greater nation.

| Level | | What grows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vyakti | The individual | Focus, discipline, calm, character |
| Parivar | The family | Respect, time together, shared values |
| Samaj | The community | Seva, harmony, neighbours who care |
| Rashtra | The nation | Pride, responsibility, Viksit Bharat 2047 |

Every topic is seen through four pillars: **Wisdom** (*jñāna*), **Life management** (*swadharma*), **Human management** (*sambandha*) and **Complete management** (*rājadharma*).

## A gathering morning (9:30 am to 12:30 pm)

| Time | Segment | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9:30 | Deep prajwalan | Invited delegates (for example an ambassador or guest of honour) open the gathering, lighting the lamp with a child and an elder; children chant the invocation |
| 9:45 | Pravachan | A volunteer presents the day's topic, with one practice everyone does together |
| 10:30 | Vishram | A short break |
| 10:45 | Shishya sabha | Four circles with activities and games: Bal Gurukul (children), Yuva Sabha (youth and families), Karma Sabha (professionals and civic leaders), Vriddha Sabha (elders) |
| 11:30 | Quiz | A quick quiz on the day's topic for everyone |
| 11:45 | Prashnottari | The life question from the Ramayana or Mahabharata; open questions, children first |
| 12:10 | Sanskriti | Bhajan, dance or skit |
| 12:25 | Sankalp and certificates | The month's home practice, and a certificate for everyone who took part |

## Resources: the volunteer model

The venue, sound, durries and chairs are donated or lent. The only cash needs are printing, certificates and children's materials, about ₹3,500 a gathering, met from a voluntary Seva Kosh box whose accounts are shared openly every quarter. Money is never sought from businesses.

## Making it attract everyone

- **Give everyone a role**, not just a talk to attend: children recite and play, youth run the quiz and bhajan jam, elders tell stories, professionals discuss real cases.
- **Keep the thread alive between Sundays:** verse of the month on WhatsApp (week 1), a family small experiment (week 2), a youth-led bhajan jam or heritage walk (week 3), a personal invitation from a lane volunteer (week 4).
- **Invite through people, not just messages.** One volunteer per building or lane personally invites 5–10 families; WhatsApp is the backup. Plan seating for about 70% of RSVPs.
- **One big day in December:** a Ramayana–Mahabharata day with costumes, the youth quiz, elders' storytelling and the year's certificates.

## Safeguarding

Every volunteer signs a code of conduct. No volunteer is ever alone with a child. Photos only with written consent, and no child is named or tagged online. One named person handles concerns.
